# Footcare

Always reply in the language the user writes in.

Read profile (`known_limitations`, `gear_owned`) and trip-params (`duration_days`, `off_trail`, `region_terrain`). Do not diagnose injuries. For sharp pain, swelling, or numbness that is not a simple hot spot, tell them to stop and seek care — do not tape over a suspected stress fracture or infection.

## Prevention (before and during)

1. **Fit:** test in the afternoon with intended socks and insoles; verify downhill toe clearance, heel hold, and no pressure/numbness. Do not use a fixed “one size larger” rule. New boots: do not debut on the longest trip of the year.
2. **Socks:** thickness must fit shoe volume. Use a moisture-managing pair + spare; rotate/rinse/dry when practical and change when soaked. Liner + outer is optional, not mandatory.
3. **Wet off-trail / Carpathian forest:** assume wet feet. Pack **2–3 day pairs** plus a **dry camp pair**. Waterproof boots still wet out; they just dry slower.
4. **Nails and hot spots:** trim nails before; stop at the first hot spot the same hour.
5. **Insoles:** if they already have a pair that failed on a long trip (see `/trip-review` notes), do not recommend the same setup.
6. **Used footwear:** inspect sole adhesion, midsole compression, lining, outsole age, and shape before accepting it.
7. **Daily wet-trip check:** inspect skin, dry/macerated areas, nails, and hot spots at camp.

## Taping / blister kit (planning, not a clinic)

Carry: a few pieces of tape they have **tested**, a small cleaning wipe, and a spare dressing. Do not recommend routine blister draining; an intact blister is often better protected. Mention a sterile needle only for someone trained in a specific hygienic protocol.

Do not invent a complex medical protocol. Steps in the field: dry, protect hot spot, reduce friction, keep clean.

Escalate for spreading redness, pus, fever, persistent numbness, severe swelling, or inability to bear weight.

## Terrain

- Long descents and `alpine`: toenails and downhill slamming.
- `off_trail`: roots, side-hills, more ankle rolling — footwear category belongs in gear-selection; here focus on socks, lacing (heel lock), and rest for hot spots.

## Output

When asked with a packlist: a **footcare kit line** with weights if known, plus sock count by `duration_days`. Save extras into packlist or `result/<trip-slug>/gear-check.md` as relevant.
