# Camp hygiene and sanitation

Always reply in the language the user writes in.

Read profile dietary/footcare needs and trip fields for duration, water, waste rules, weather, bugs, and campsite constraints. Local regulations override generic practice.

## Hand and food hygiene

- Wash hands after toileting and before food preparation. Sanitizer supplements washing; it is less effective on visibly dirty hands and against some pathogens.
- Keep soap, toothpaste, food scraps, and wash water away from natural water.
- Separate raw/dirty tasks from eating gear. Protect food from heat, animals, and contaminated water.

## Toileting and waste

- Follow the recorded `waste_disposal_rules`.
- Where catholes are permitted, a common starting rule is 15–20 cm deep and at least 60 m / 200 ft from water, trails, and camp. Alpine, desert, winter, high-use, or protected areas may require packing everything out.
- Pack out wet wipes, hygiene products, menstrual products, and all synthetic waste. Toilet paper rules vary.
- Never present leaves or stones over surface waste as adequate disposal.

## Washing and personal needs

- Use small-volume washing away from water and disperse wastewater only where rules allow.
- Carry dental care, chafe prevention, menstrual/personal supplies, and a pack-out bag as applicable.
- Wet wipes and “dry shower” products create waste; include their pack-out weight.
- Keep feet, folds, and chafe-prone areas clean/dry when practical; route foot problems to `.agents/skills/footcare/SKILL.md`.

## Conditions

- Sparse water: prioritize hands, food handling, feet, and chafe areas; do not consume emergency drinking reserve for comfort washing.
- Cold/wet: preserve the dry sleep system and perform hygiene before changing into dry layers.
- High bug pressure: shelter/repellent choices must follow local safety and environmental rules.

## Output

Integrate hygiene items and local waste rules into `packlist.md` and sanitation/water-contamination risks into `risk-assessment.md`. Do not diagnose or prescribe medication.
