# Nutrition reference tables

Use with [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Numbers are planning heuristics, not lab values.

For weight-sensitive multi-day food, an overall average near **4–5 kcal/g (400–500 kcal/100 g)** is a practical target. Short or comfort-first trips may carry lower-density food. Product labels override this table.

## Typical trail densities (kcal / 100 g)

| Food | Approx kcal/100 g | Notes |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| Olive oil | 880 | Tiny bottle, huge calories |
| Nut butter | 580–650 | Squeeze pouch |
| Nuts / mixed | 550–650 | Easy overeat salt |
| Dark chocolate | 500–580 | Melts in summer |
| Hard cheese | 350–400 | Keep cool; wrap well |
| Dried sausage | 400–500 | Salt bomb; check fat in heat |
| Energy bars | 350–450 | Check fiber/sugar |
| Tortillas / crispbread | 300–400 | Durable |
| Instant oats | 350–380 | Needs water |
| Pasta / couscous dry | 350–370 | Needs fuel + water |
| Freeze-dried dinner (dry) | 350–450 | Check sodium |
| Dried fruit | 250–320 | Fiber + GI |
| Jerky | 250–300 | Protein, salty |

## Meal water (typical)

| Item | Water to rehydrate |
|------|-------------------|
| Freeze-dried dinner | 300–500 ml |
| Instant oats | 200–300 ml |
| Couscous | 1:1 by volume as a start |

Track planned boil and simmer time; simmer-heavy grains cost much more fuel than soak/rehydrate meals. Couscous, instant grains, and pre-soaking reduce fuel use.

## Plan checks

- Daily kcal range and packed grams.
- Overall kcal/g and protein estimate when body weight is known.
- Sodium from actual labels and expected sweat/heat.
- Water for drinking + cooking.
- Planned boils/simmers and fuel.
- Tested no-cook emergency meal.
- Hot-weather storage: opened meat/dairy, cheese softening, chocolate melting, rancid fats.

## Cold vs heat

- **Winter / shoulder:** more fat, hot dinners every night, extra emergency calories (~400–800 kcal).
- **Summer:** chocolate and cheese fail; prefer oil, nuts, freeze-dried, dry sausage if it stays firm.

## Off-trail / Carpathian-style days

Expect slower km/h and more kcal than a marked alpine trail at the same distance. Prefer dinners that cook in one pot. Do not plan on shops inside a border or remote forest block unless `resupply_available: true`.
