# Gear reference

Use with [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Categories, not shopping SKUs.

## Base weight bands (no food, no water)

| Philosophy | Rough base |
|------------|------------|
| Ultralight | 4–7 kg |
| Traditional | 7–11 kg |
| Comfort-first | 11 kg+ |

Food is ~0.7–1.1 kg/person/day depending on density. Water is 1 kg/L carried.

## Season × sleep/shelter (minimum thinking)

| Season | Shelter | Sleep |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Summer | Tarp/tent with bug story if forest | Bag/quilt rated near expected overnight low + pad |
| Shoulder | Full fly, condensation plan | Warmer bag; pad R-value up |
| Winter | Four-season or proven snow setup | Bag + pad(s) for ground freeze; stove that works cold |

Rate the complete system from expected low + forecast margin, ISO 23537 comfort data where available, pad R-value, wind/exposure, moisture, and whether the user sleeps warm or cold. A hammock needs an underquilt or suitable tested pad.

## Sustained-rain architecture

1. Waterproof pack liner.
2. Sealed dry sleep module (insulation + sleep clothing).
3. Wet travel clothing isolated from dry items.
4. Shelter-first camp setup and dry-module-last breakdown.
5. Abort or lower-route trigger if sleeping insulation becomes wet.

A rain cover is supplemental; it does not replace an internal liner.

## Duration effects

| Days | Extra |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | Tiny cook kit or no-cook |
| 3–5 | Full cook + food bag; repair (tape, cord) |
| 6+ | More repair, spare heat source, food packaging discipline |

## Off-trail vs trail (typical deltas)

| Item | Trail | Off-trail / dense forest |
|------|-------|---------------------------|
| Footwear | Trail shoe or light boot | More protection, aggressive outsole |
| Poles | Optional-to-yes | Yes |
| Clothing | Light | Tougher shells, maybe gaiters |
| Navigation | GPX + map | Extra battery, altimeter, slower time budget |
| Gloves | Light | Thorn/veg pair optional |

## Cook systems

- Canister: simple above freezing.
- Alcohol / solid fuel: legal/fire rules and winter performance vary.
- Wood: last resort; fire bans and wet Carpathian forest make it unreliable.

Match pot size to freeze-dried vs simmered grain (nutrition skill). Estimate fuel from planned boils/simmers, starting-water temperature, wind, altitude, group size, and stove efficiency. Verify fuel compatibility and local restrictions.

**Never operate a stove inside a tent, bivy, or enclosed vestibule.**

## Equipment test

- Pack carries expected departure load without pressure/numbness.
- Shelter pitches in wind/rain; seams and guylines checked.
- Sleep system tested near expected conditions.
- Pad, stove, filter, electronics, and repair patches work.
- Critical insulation stays dry in a hose/rain simulation.
- Condition recorded: new / good / worn / repair-needed.
