# Navigation reference

Use with [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). These are screening prompts, not fixed route-speed promises.

## Terrain review

| Feature | Check | Planning effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tight contours | Slope angle, cliff bands, runout | Slower pace or reroute |
| Dense scrub / dwarf pine | Satellite texture, local reports | Very high time/energy penalty |
| Blowdown | Age, wetness, slope | Slips, snags, detours |
| Side-hill | Surface, exposure, duration | Fatigue and ankle load |
| Gully / dry stream | Entry/exit and cliffs below | Do not assume it is a descent route |
| River | Depth, speed, rain, downstream hazards | Alternate or abort |

Regional vegetation elevation bands are approximations, not universal rules. Compare several map layers and recent route observations.

## Time and daylight

- Begin with maintained-trail pace the hiker has actually sustained.
- Apply separate penalties for ascent, descent, off-trail vegetation, crossings, and pack load.
- Present a range and state uncertainty; do not use a universal multiplier.
- Set a hard turnaround and a camp-search deadline before dark.
- For difficult objectives, schedule a shakedown using the actual kit and navigation setup.

## Offline test

1. Download maps, contours, GPX, critical waypoints, and bailout routes.
2. Enable airplane mode and restart the app.
3. Confirm route, labels, contours, and saved notes still render.
4. Export a second copy in an interoperable format.
5. Test charger, cable, power bank, phone case, and cold-weather storage.
6. Verify independent backup and user competence.

## Crossing checklist

- Depth, speed, temperature, footing.
- Safe entry and exit.
- No strainers, waterfalls, constrictions, or deep pool immediately downstream.
- Recent rain, snowmelt, and upstream catchment.
- Pack/buckle management appropriate to the crossing and training.
- Alternate route and turnaround threshold.

Never practise in high-consequence water. Use controlled, low-risk training with qualified instruction where available.

## Source-verification record

For weather, hydrology, permits, borders, parks, camping, fire, and drone rules record:

- authority / source;
- URL or contact;
- checked date;
- effective date;
- unresolved questions.

User-generated maps, social media, Telegram channels, and detention reports are not legal authority.
